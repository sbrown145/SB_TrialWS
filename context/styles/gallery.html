Style Library — sixteen boilerplates

Sixteen distinct, paste-into-your-agent design systems, each documented as a self-contained sheet in the same seven-section format. Every panel below renders the same little composition — kicker, headline, body, chip, button — so the difference you see is the system, not the content.

To use one: copy its NN-name.md into your repo as STYLE.md and tell your agent "Follow STYLE.md for all styling." The full comparison lives in 00-MASTER-BREAKDOWN.md.
